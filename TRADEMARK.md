# Openwater Trademark Policy

> **STATUS: DRAFT / POLICY STUB — pending review and finalization by counsel.**
> This document states the intended structure of the Openwater trademark and certification marks so
> engineering and community work can proceed. The binding terms are set by the Certification
> Agreement maintained in
> [`openwater-certification`](https://github.com/OpenwaterHealth/openwater-certification). Where this
> stub and the executed Agreement differ, the Agreement controls.

## Why this exists

Openwater's extension layer is licensed under Apache-2.0, which by design permits proprietary
downstream use. Apache alone gives Openwater almost no control over how deployments represent
themselves. The trademark and certification program restore that control **without using the
copyright license to do it.**

## The two designations

| Mark | Who may use it | Meaning |
|------|----------------|---------|
| **Openwater** (unqualified) | **Reserved to Openwater Health, Inc. only.** | Openwater's own products and official releases. |
| **Openwater-Certified** | Third-party deployments that meet program requirements and sign the Certification Agreement. | A procurement-grade signal that a deployment conforms to Openwater's schemas, safety, and terms. |

Unqualified use of "Openwater" by a third party — in a product name, service name, or in a way that
implies Openwater endorsement — is **not permitted** without a separate written agreement.

## Certification tracks (under "Openwater-Certified")

| Track | Scope | Additional requirements |
|-------|-------|-------------------------|
| **Standard** | Default commercial and research deployments | Schema conformance; registry entry |
| **Clinical** | FDA-regulated clinical deployments | Independent annual third-party audit |
| **Veterinary** | SecureDX-aligned veterinary workflows | Species-specific conformance addenda |

## Permitted / not permitted (summary — see the Agreement for controlling terms)

**Permitted**
- Truthful statements that a deployment is "Openwater-Certified" once certification is granted and
  while it remains active.
- Nominative references (e.g., "compatible with the Openwater Device Data Specification").

**Not permitted**
- Using "Openwater" (unqualified) as, or within, a third-party product or company name.
- Claiming or implying certification that has not been granted, or after it has lapsed or been revoked.
- Modifying the Openwater marks or using them in a way that implies endorsement beyond the granted track.

## Enforcement

Certification and the right to use "Openwater-Certified" are contractual and revocable. The
Certification Agreement includes a liquidated-damages provision (**US $25,000 per device** for
willful violations, per the June 24, 2026 architecture) as the remedy against determined bad actors.
